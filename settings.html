Install on iPhone / iPad

Apple iOS requires adding apps directly through Safari. Follow these 4 easy steps:

1
Open this website in Safari browser (if you are in Chrome or another app, copy the URL to Safari).
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of the Safari screen .
3
Scroll down the options list and tap Add to Home Screen .
4
Tap Add in the top right corner. The Iqbal Fashion ERP icon will now be on your Home Screen!
